**OverRez {version}: Beyond Ultra.**

OverRez is New Game+ for your graphics. It layers real-time frame generation, super resolution, neural rendering and color grading on top of any PC game by capturing your screen. Nothing is injected into the game or its memory.

What's in {version}:

- **Screen capture only.** Capture a game window or a whole monitor. Video files, capture cards, PS5, Xbox and PC streaming, network sources and export have been removed to keep OverRez focused and light.
- **Game mode.** Mouse passthrough for PC and 3D games: choose a borderless game window or monitor, press **Home** for adjustments and **Ctrl+Alt+Home** to leave.
- **Enhancement.** Denoising, super resolution, frame generation, color grading and the node editor, using the NVIDIA and AMD runtimes included in each package.
- **Capture your play.** Screenshots, recording, Instant Replay and live translation captions.
- **Streaming compatibility.** The OverRez picture is available to OBS and other streaming apps through the virtual camera (install it from Settings), with OBS game capture compatibility settings.
- **Classic look only,** with no startup animation or music.

Choose the package for your GPU (NVIDIA or AMD). The Setup EXE installs for your Windows account; the portable ZIP runs from any writable folder via **Veyra.exe**. OverRez installs separately from Veyra and keeps its own settings.

The installers are not code-signed yet. Automatic update checks are off for now; new versions appear on this repository's Releases page.

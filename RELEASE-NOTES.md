**OverRez {version}: Beyond Ultra.**

OverRez is New Game+ for your graphics. It layers real-time frame generation, super resolution, neural rendering and color grading on top of any PC game by capturing your screen. Nothing is injected into the game or its memory.

What's in {version}:

- **Opens straight to screen capture.** Pick a game window or a whole display and press Start capture.
- **Game mode is always on.** The enhanced picture covers the game while the game keeps your keyboard, mouse and controller. Press **Home** to adjust the picture and **Ctrl+Alt+Home** to stop. Run games in borderless windowed mode.
- **Enhancement.** Denoising, anti-aliasing, super resolution, RTX Video HDR, frame generation, color grading and the node editor, using the NVIDIA and AMD runtimes included in each package.
- **Capture your play.** Screenshots, recording and Instant Replay, now with your PC's sound, plus live translation captions.
- **Streaming compatibility.** The OverRez picture is available to OBS and other streaming apps as **OverRez Virtual Camera** (install it from Settings > Video), with OBS game capture compatibility settings.
- **Focused.** Video files, capture cards, console and PC streaming, network sources and export have been removed. English only, classic look, no startup animation or music.

Choose the package for your GPU (NVIDIA or AMD). The Setup EXE installs for your Windows account; the portable ZIP runs from any writable folder via **OverRez.exe**. OverRez keeps its own settings, separate from Veyra.

The installers are not code-signed yet. Automatic update checks are off for now; new versions appear on this repository's Releases page.

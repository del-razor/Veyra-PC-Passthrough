Veyra PLUS {version} includes every change in Veyra {version}, plus:

- **Screen Capture → Game mode is kept.** Mouse passthrough for PC and 3D games works as it did in 2.2.0: choose a borderless game window or monitor, press **Home** for adjustments and **Ctrl+Alt+Home** to leave.
- **Classic look only.** The app always uses the Classic colour scheme, with no startup animation or startup music. The Dusk scheme and Startup music settings are removed.
- **Separate install.** Veyra PLUS installs as its own program, so it can sit next to an official Veyra install.

Choose the package for the GPU Veyra will use (NVIDIA or AMD). The Setup EXE installs for your Windows account; the portable ZIP runs from any writable folder via **Veyra.exe**.

Not included yet: PS5 Remote Play, Xbox and Moonlight streaming, and Magewell capture-card support. The installers are not code-signed, and the in-app update check still looks at the original Veyra releases.
